## Bugs found and fixed, in order

Each of these was found on hardware except the first, which `replay` caught
before anything drove.

1. **Pure pursuit drives away at 180 deg of bearing error.** Curvature goes to
   zero when the target is directly behind, so it steers nothing. Caught in
   simulation on the first `replay` run.
2. **Turn direction inverted in align.** Bearing increases clockwise, so a
   positive error means turn right - a negative rate. Error grew 13 -> 77 deg
   over 12 s of turning the wrong way.
3. **Turn direction flipped on noise near 180 deg.** Sign of the error is
   decided by jitter at the antipode. 105 s stuck oscillating at +/-179 deg.
   Direction is now latched.
4. **Turn rates below the rotational breakaway.** The final turn commanded
   120 mrad/s against a floor of ~200. It could never have moved.
5. **Creep steering inverted, and then removed entirely.** Same sign error,
   but with no feedback to the target it integrated: heading ran 3.7 -> 180
   deg and the machine crept away for over a metre. Steering during creep is
   now off - the angular floor makes fine correction impossible anyway.
6. **Pure pursuit chasing noise near the target.** At 0.4 m, 2 cm of RTK
   jitter is 2.9 deg of bearing. Heading diverged -0.6 -> +10.1 deg over the
   last 70 cm. The approach line is now locked at 1.2 m.
7. **Crab during the blind run.** Locking at 2 m and driving straight gave
   8 cm of cross-track drift. Lateral correction now stays on until 30 cm.
8. **Speed stepped instead of ramping.** A 500 -> 40 mm/s step took ~0.5 s and
   20 cm, so the machine entered the creep zone still at 490 mm/s. Speed now
   ramps from 1.2 m.
9. **Controlling the antenna, not the vehicle.** The mast is 0.8 m ahead of
   the rotation centre, so squaring up swung the controlled point through an
   arc - 117 cm after a 167 deg turn. All control now runs on the rotation
   centre.
10. **Two processes reading one serial port.** The forwarder and the
    controller each got a fraction of the bytes. The forwarder now owns both
    ports and republishes state.
11. **Final status line discarded.** The control loop broke on completion
    before printing, throwing away the arrival numbers.
12. **`fh` shadowed** in `mission` - file handle and final heading.

## Lessons that shaped the design

**The machine has no fine control authority.** 40 mm/s minimum linear,
~200 mrad/s minimum angular. Any control law assuming small corrections
oscillates. Hence: latched turn direction, no creep steering, pulsed final
turn, and a minimum magnitude applied to every non-zero angular command.

**Three separate places converted a heading error into an angular rate and
two were backwards.** That should be one function with a test.

**A control phase that cannot see its own target needs a sanity bound.** The
locked approach has no feedback, so a fault integrates. Added abort guards
on heading drift and overshoot.
