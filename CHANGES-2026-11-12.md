# Changes, 12 Nov 2026

Controller tuning after the first ground-truth serpentine in College Station
(2 rows, 4 plots, 2 headland points, mission completed unattended), a pivot
test and three yaw-tests. Proven in a simulator calibrated against that
day's field data; not yet driven.

## What the field showed

| Mark | At arrival | After final turn | Final turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| plot_001 | 2.7 cm | 7.1 cm | ~9 deg |
| plot_002-004 | 2.4-2.8 | 2.8 | ~1 deg |
| mark_5 (headland) | 2.2 | 47 cm | ~88 deg |
| mark_8 (headland) | 3.6 | 10.9 cm | ~39 deg |

GPS-reported.

1. **Turning in place on turf walks the machine backward ~0.28 m per
   radian.** plot_001 4.4 cm / 0.16 rad, mark_5 43 cm / 1.54 rad, and a
   pendant pivot test (12 and 35 cm for two ~95 deg turns). Not the antenna
   offset: the pivot test's two turns fit offset errors of +5 and -2 cm,
   inconsistent, with most of the movement unexplained.
2. **Plots 2-4 settled at +2.7 to +2.8 cm cross**, just under the 3 cm
   steering deadband.
3. **The wheels obey turn commands; the turf does not.** yaw-test with the
   logger running alongside, ~300 mm/s: wheels reached the commanded rate
   (0x18E) in ~1 s and wound down over ~1 s, but GPS heading turned at only
   **~10-25% of the wheel rate, about the same left and right** (three
   yaw-tests; one left turn stalled mid-way and read 9% over its steady
   part, which was first misread as a left/right asymmetry). Jerky: the
   heading stalled for ~2.4 s mid-turn, then jumped. At 600 mrad/s and
   300 mm/s the inside wheels stop and it pivots. In-place turns in the
   mission ran ~4 deg/s against 260-350 mrad/s commanded.

The first yaw-tests (2 s turns) read 10-20% and were taken at first as lag;
the logged wheel rate showed it is mostly scrub.

## What changed (defaults)

**Cross-track deadband 3 -> 1.5 cm** (`CROSS_CORRECT_M`).

**Post-turn check and creep fix.** After the final turn: stand 0.5 s, average
the readings, and if the along-row error is over 1.5 cm, creep straight
forward or back at 40 mm/s to fix it (up to 2 tries, never more than
25 cm - beyond that it is reported, not chased). Sideways error cannot be
fixed without turning and is reported. The arrival line now reads
`squared up: arrived X cm, after turn Y cm, now along A cross C cm`.

**Smoothed stopping.** Arrival and creep-fix decisions use the mean of the
last 3 distinct GPS readings, not the first single reading under 1 cm.

**Overshoot guard** compares against the closest approach so far.

**`Rover.send()` floor split into in-place and rolling**
(`ANGULAR_MIN_ROLLING_MRAD_S`, above 100 mm/s). Both 220; the rolling one
is now separately tunable.

**New: `amiga_nav.py yaw-test`.** Drives straight and holds turn commands,
alternating left/right, and fits the yaw achieved. Run `rover_log.py drive`
alongside it to also capture the wheel rate.

## Built, tested, and left OFF

**Row-line following** (`ROW_LINE_FOLLOW = False`): follow the line through
the mark along its taught heading, pure pursuit to a point 2 m ahead on it.
It needs turning authority the machine does not have on turf: in
simulation it ended 4-6 cm off sideways on the 12 Nov route against under
2 cm for the default, at 12%, 18% and 25% turning efficiency alike. A 0.6 m lookahead oscillated (27 cm overshoot) - the machine
rolls ~0.6 m before a turn takes effect.

**Steering gain and rolling cap** (`STEER_YAW_GAIN = 1.0`,
`TURN_PER_SPEED_MAX = 99`): scaling pure pursuit up for scrub. No effect
with steering at the mark, because the 220 floor already commands far more
than pure pursuit asks. A low-speed steering gate (`STEER_MIN_SPEED_MM_S`)
likewise made things worse and is 0.

## Simulation

The simulator (`test_controller_tuning.py`) models: pivot slip 0.28 m/rad
backward; no in-place rotation below 200 mrad/s; rolling wheel-rate ramp
~1 s; 18% rolling yaw efficiency both ways, 25% in place;
stick-slip (efficiency varies 0.5-1.5x per half second); 2% leftward crab;
GPS at 10 Hz. Calibrated by replaying the 12 Nov route (marks rebuilt from
the run log) with the OLD controller until it matched the field: plots
within +/-3 cm cross, mark_5 slip 31-39 cm vs 43 measured.

Mean / max over 8 seeds (cm):

| | 12 Nov route, plots: along | cross | First plot: along | cross |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| old controller | 2.0 / 4.5 | 1.9 / 4.3 | 1.8 / 3.6 | 2.3 / 3.2 |
| new defaults | 0.6 / 2.0 | 0.7 / 1.9 | 0.8 / 1.9 | 0.7 / 1.3 |
| row-line on | 0.3 / 1.0 | 3.9 / 6.0 | 0.3 / 0.9 | 2.6 / 4.8 |

The new defaults also beat the old at 12% and 25% turning efficiency, and
with a deliberately lopsided 9% / 37% model.

The sim does not reproduce plot_001's 8 deg approach angle or its 7.1 cm -
the field start differed. The tape is the judge.
